Our Story

Pacific Trails Resort was created with a simple vision: to offer a place where luxury and nature exist in perfect balance. Nestled along the rugged California coastline, our resort was built for travelers who want to escape the noise of everyday life and reconnect with something real.

What started as a small coastal retreat has grown into a destination known for its peaceful atmosphere, breathtaking ocean views, and personalized guest experience. Every yurt is carefully designed to provide comfort without taking away from the natural beauty that surrounds it.

Our team is passionate about creating unforgettable stays—from sunrise hikes along the cliffs to evenings spent relaxing by the lodge. At Pacific Trails, it’s not just about where you stay, but how you feel while you’re here.

We believe in slowing down, appreciating the outdoors, and giving every guest a space to recharge. Whether you're here for adventure or relaxation, our goal is to make your experience feel effortless, personal, and memorable.

Our Values

  • Preserving the natural beauty of the coastline
  • Providing a peaceful and relaxing guest experience
  • Delivering high-quality, personalized service
  • Creating a balance between comfort and adventure
